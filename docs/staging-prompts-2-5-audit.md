18 October 2026. Production unchanged; no merge is authorised.

The messages available in this chat label **Part 2** and **Part 3**, but do not
explicitly label Prompts 4 and 5. This checklist maps 2 = supplied cleaning scope,
3 = client decisions, 4 = payment rules and clarifications, 5 = assignment,
unmatched-visit recovery and the final late-payment window. It covers each supplied
requirement, with later answers taking precedence over earlier ones. It does not
invent the unavailable Part 1 table or apply the held legal patch.

“Done on staging” means the feature/content is present and the verification described
below passed. It does not claim every screen was manually exercised on a physical
Android phone, nor that a queued email was delivered. Scope/checklist/photo rules in
Part 2 are published instructions, not a new mandatory persisted checklist workflow.
